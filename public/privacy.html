AdVoid for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

AdVoid does not operate analytics, advertising, telemetry, or user-account servers. The Android app displays YouTube in a local WebView and applies its playback and filtering features on your device.

Who operates AdVoid

AdVoid is developed and maintained by Eldar Najaf. A dedicated privacy and support email will be listed here and in Google Play before the app enters closed testing.

Information AdVoid handles

AdVoid itself does not ask you for a name, email address, phone number, payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or camera access. The app requests only Android internet access.

If you sign in to YouTube, Google and YouTube may process your account information, viewing activity, searches, identifiers, cookies, and other data under their own policies. AdVoid does not receive a separate copy of that account data.

On-device storage

The Android WebView may store cookies, site data, cache, preferences, and browsing state on your device so YouTube can work and keep you signed in. You can remove this local information through Android Settings by clearing AdVoid's storage or uninstalling the app.

Network services

Video pages, media, search results, live chat, account sign-in, and related content are delivered by YouTube and Google. Their use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the applicable YouTube terms. Links you open may be governed by the destination site's privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

AdVoid does not sell personal information. AdVoid does not share personal information with the developer or independent advertising or analytics companies. Data sent directly to YouTube, Google, or a link you choose to open is handled by that service as described above.

Children

AdVoid is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. YouTube's own age requirements and supervised experiences continue to apply.

Security and changes

AdVoid starts its built-in web experience at YouTube over HTTPS and opens this privacy-policy link over HTTPS. Links selected inside web content may be handled by their destination. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or legal requirements change; the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AdVoid maintainers through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
